# Learning Journal

Write a short entry at the end of every study session. Five minutes, three questions. It feels unnecessary right up until:

- a concept you "learned" three weeks ago has vanished (re-reading your own words brings it back fast),
- an interviewer asks "tell me about a difficult bug you solved" (your story bank is here),
- you feel like you're not progressing (scroll up and look at how far you've come).


## Bug story bank

When you solve a bug that took you more than 30 minutes, write it up here. These become interview answers.

```md
### Title (e.g. "The interval that counted from zero forever")
- **Situation:** what I was building
- **Problem:** what went wrong (symptom)
- **Investigation:** what I tried, what I ruled out, how I found it
- **Root cause:** why it actually happened
- **Fix & lesson:** what I changed and what I do differently now
```
